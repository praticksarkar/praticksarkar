Astroparticle Physics · IACS Kolkata

Reading dark matter's shadow in light, gas, and spacetime.

I am a PhD candidate at the Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science, working under Prof. Sourov Roy. My thesis probes axion-like particles and dark matter through astrophysical observables — from the photon ring of a black hole to the gravitational-wave hum left by ultralight fields.

What the thesis is actually about

Photon rings

ALP detection at the edge of a black hole's shadow

Using the Event Horizon Telescope's image of M87*, I study how an axion-like particle background would leave its fingerprint on the photon ring — the thin bright halo of light bent almost all the way around the black hole.

Spectroscopy

eV-scale ALP dark matter from M87 galaxy spectra

Spectroscopic observations of the M87 galaxy constrain how eV-scale axion-like particles couple to photons, using the Raffelt–Stodolski conversion framework against different dark-matter density profiles.

Gravitational waves

Ultralight dark matter in the stochastic GW background

Pulsar timing array data (NANOGrav, EPTA, PPTA) can carry the imprint of self-interacting ultralight dark matter — I work out how soliton cores and the cusp–core problem shape that signature.
